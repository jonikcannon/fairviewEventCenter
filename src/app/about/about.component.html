WHO WE ARE

Faith and community, together.

Greater Harvest Church calls the Fairview Community Center home, sharing this space with the neighbors, families, and groups who gather here throughout the week.

Our congregation gathers for worship, fellowship, and service, while the community center opens its doors to weddings, celebrations, meetings, and events of every kind.

Whether you're joining us for a service or booking the space for your own event, we'd love to welcome you.
